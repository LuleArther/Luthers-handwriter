How to use

1

Write or paste

Type in the editor. Use headings, bold, lists, colours and alignment — they all carry over to the handwriting.

2

Pick the look

Choose paper, a handwriting font (or upload your own), margins and how messy it should be. Photo makes it look like a picture of a page on a desk.

3

Download

Get every page as a PDF or a ZIP of images, or just the page you're looking at. “New variation” rewrites it with fresh randomness.
